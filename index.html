Livingston Ecolancers

CHIEF ENGINEER / PRESIDENTSHELL ECO-MARATHON AMERICAS2023–2026

I helped rebuild this team from nothing. Go-Kart Design & Fabrication

INDEPENDENT PROJECTLIVINGSTON, NJ2020–PRESENT

This build has been running since 7th grade. It started as a modified shopping-cart frame, became a self-welded frame in 8th grade, and has been a purpose-built race chassis since freshman year — through four engine platforms, ending in a 250cc dual-cylinder Kawasaki Ninja engine. It reaches 60+ mph with ease, with a calculated top speed of 85 mph.

The hardest problem I've solved on it: I first adapted the donor Ninja wiring harness onto the kart, keeping every original feature working — the horn, the dash gauges — then went back and reverse-engineered and optimized the entire harness, stripping it down from a consistent 0.75" bundle to under 0.5" and retaping it by hand. I've also diagnosed and repaired two structural failures under load: a bent frame (heated, reshaped, and reinforced) and a snapped shifter linkage, redesigned into something stronger.
